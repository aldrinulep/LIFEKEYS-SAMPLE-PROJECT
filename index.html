Regulatory & Compliance Alignment

Mapping Lifekeys support metrics directly to International Labour Organization (ILO) Maritime Labour Convention (MLC) 2006 standards and IMO frameworks.

MLC A2.3 / STCW A-VIII/1

Rest and Fatigue Management

Support: Fatigue education & aggregate context.
Evidence: Work/rest breach logs, circumstances, Chief Officer review logs.

MLC A4.3(1)(a) / ISO 45003

Safety and Psychosocial Risk

Support: Organizational surveys & awareness.
Evidence: SMS risk records, mitigation controls, and designated owner follow-up.

MLC A3.1(17)

Recreation & Connection

Support: Activity planning and participation tracking.
Evidence: Dated events, distinct participants, duty/rest availability logs.

MLC A4.1

Professional Support

Support: Psychological support and confidential referral routes.
Evidence: Current access channels and incident follow-up (excluding clinical details).

MLC A5.1.5

Protected Reporting

Support: Awareness of onboard grievance channels.
Evidence: Complaint procedures and communication records (check-ins ≠ complaint channels).

Referenced Only (Outside Scope)

Living & Employment Conditions

Support: Flag aggregate feedback to responsible shore teams.
Evidence: Accommodation, catering, and employment action tracking records. Note: Lifekeys does not certify living conditions.

Review Workflow & Action Ownership

To maintain data integrity before executive client release, the following protocol is enforced:

  • Shore Analyst Verification: Reconcile missing vessel data prior to calculating fleet-wide averages.
  • Chief Officer Review: Evaluate port-call workload associations before subsequent close-call berthing sequences.
  • Action Tracking: Assign unique Action IDs, owners, due dates, and closure evidence for all identified follow-ups.
