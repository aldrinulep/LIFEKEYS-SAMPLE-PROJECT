04 / GOVERNANCE BY DESIGN

Privacy and review controls

Proposed safeguards informed by my DICT Privacy Impact Assessment and Data Privacy training. Production decisions require an agreed purpose, lawful basis, responsibilities and retention schedule.

Separate datasets

Voluntary wellbeing responses would be kept separate from named work/rest and personnel records. Client reports would receive approved aggregates. Clinical content and individual support histories would not appear in these reports.

Limit small-group disclosure

Proposed starting rule: suppress a subgroup below five distinct contributors. Also assess rare categories, totals that reveal a hidden cell, repeated reports and combined filters. Five is a design assumption for review, not a legal guarantee of anonymity.

Define access and retention

An analyst prepares aggregates; a designated reviewer approves releases; clients receive the agreed report. Retention and deletion rules would be defined per dataset before live collection, with access logged and periodically reviewed.

Protect voluntary participation

Non-response and choosing not to attend would not be treated as poor performance. Self-reported feelings are not clinical assessments. Existing support and complaint routes must remain clear and accessible.

Field handling and acceptance criteria
  • Dates and scope: use an agreed vessel reporting-day convention, month and cutoff. Validate that each included source covers the same period.
  • Missing and zero: missing is null / pending. A reported zero is a valid observation. A zero denominator produces “Not applicable”, never 0%.
  • Duplicates: the collection layer should allow one valid check-in per eligible crew-day using an appropriately protected mechanism; retain a documented correction policy. Never claim this aggregate demo proves row-level uniqueness.
  • Rounding: calculate from full counts, then display one decimal. Show numerator and denominator.
  • Acceptance examples: two missing submissions keep completeness at 10/12; their crew-days do not enter reply coverage. A duplicated submission is rejected. A mismatch in the event balance blocks release.
  • Disclosure: run subgroup and inference-risk checks before export. Source provenance and reviewer sign-off remain required.

06 / CLIENT SUPPORT & IMPROVEMENT

Client review pack

Example answers for an internal commercial or client-review team. Proposed scope and service capabilities would be confirmed with Lifekeys before use in a live proposal.

Does this demonstrate MLC compliance?

It offers structured supporting evidence about service access, engagement and follow-up. It does not certify a vessel or replace statutory records. Start with the relevant requirement, show the approved service scope, then connect each claim to operator-held evidence and the reporting specification.

Review references: Compliance support map; S01–S05; operator's applicable records.

Why is reply coverage 76.7% when two vessels are missing?

The denominator includes 7,200 eligible crew-days from the ten reporting vessels. The two missing submissions remain visible in the separate 10/12 reporting measure. This is a partial view, with follow-up pending; it cannot represent confirmed reply behaviour across all twelve vessels.

Evidence: S01–S02; ACT-01.

Does higher use mean wellbeing has improved?

No. Usage, learning completion and attendance measure engagement. An outcome evaluation would need a defined measure, appropriate consent and governance, comparable follow-up periods and review of changes in respondents and operational exposure. Even an observed change would not by itself establish causation.

What would a proposal commit to?

Proposed deliverable: a monthly aggregate report showing coverage, service engagement, operational context, evidence references and assigned follow-ups. Agree the eligible population, source systems, metric definitions, privacy controls, review responsibilities and delivery schedule during discovery. Confirm technical feasibility before promising automated imports, clinical results or certification.
